One line, at most 200 characters, about a small good thing that happened today —
something that would make a reader smile. Entry for the
[make.fun bounty "Make the Judge Smile"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-make-the-judge-smile-9228676d).

## The entry

> I dropped my keys in a puddle this morning, and a kid in yellow boots fished them out with a stick — then charged me exactly one high-five.

A child helps a stranger, freely — then the rescue is paid for in a currency
nobody expects. Warmth, a surprise turn in the ending, and four concrete small
details (puddle, yellow boots, a stick, one high-five). Ten candidate lines
were written and scored against the judging criteria; the critique and the
decision are in [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md).

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13 standard library only, deterministic, no network)
writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
for local runs) and self-checks the competition gate first: UTF-8 text,
1–200 characters, not only whitespace, a single line.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
cat out/entry.txt
```
